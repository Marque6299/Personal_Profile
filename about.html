I work where reporting meets web development.

I’m a Lead Data Analytics Specialist and web developer dedicated to modernizing operational workflows. I turn complex organizational data into clear, actionable reporting interfaces that streamline daily decisions.

My work sits between three tools. Power BI handles the modeling, custom vanilla JavaScript tools handle the interface, and Power Automate handles notifications, scheduled reports and status updates.

At WNS Global Services I lead end-to-end operational data reporting and HR analytics for site operations in Iloilo and Manila. That includes team performance dashboards and the multi-tier incentive calculation engines behind the A-Team Incentive Plan.

Alongside that, I develop web tools for the MIS Digital Transformation Initiative and connect them directly to Power BI Fabric semantic models and Databricks data layers.
